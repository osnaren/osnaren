# Portfolio brief

This repository powers Obuli Sai Naren's personal portfolio and professional profile. It brings engineering experience, product work, research, public datasets, and a printable resume into one verifiable place.

## Audience

The portfolio is designed for hiring managers, recruiters, engineering leaders, prospective collaborators, and peers who want to understand the work quickly and inspect the evidence behind it.

## Design principles

- Make projects, outcomes, and supporting links easy to find.
- Let clarity and craft communicate confidence.
- Keep the personal voice visible across professional surfaces.
- Use interaction and motion when they improve orientation or understanding.
- Prefer durable, accessible patterns over decorative effects.

## Accessibility and performance

The site targets WCAG 2.2 AA with strong contrast, semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, useful accessible names, logical reading order, and comfortable touch targets. It respects reduced-motion preferences and keeps the resume complete and readable in print and PDF output.

Performance work prioritizes stable layouts, responsive media, efficient loading, and smooth interactions across device and network conditions.
