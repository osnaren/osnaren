# Product

<!-- cspell:words WCAG -->

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, engineering leaders, prospective collaborators, and peers evaluating Obuli Sai Naren's experience, work, and professional credibility.

## Product Purpose

Present a clear, memorable personal brand for a frontend engineer. The portfolio should make professional experience, technical judgment, research, public datasets, and selected work easy to understand and verify. Success means visitors quickly recognize both engineering depth and care for user experience, then confidently continue to projects, the story, the resume, or direct contact.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, and considered. Confident without exaggeration; distinctive without becoming decorative; curious while remaining credible.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page patterns, stock portfolio templates, ornamental resume layouts, excessive iconography, gratuitous animation, and visual effects that compete with the work. Do not make the experience feel corporate, anonymous, or generated from a design trend.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence easy to find and verify.
- Practice the accessibility and performance standards the portfolio advocates.
- Let clarity and craft communicate confidence.
- Use interaction and motion only when they improve orientation or understanding.
- Keep the personal voice visible across every professional surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong contrast, semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, useful accessible names, logical reading order, and comfortable touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve complete, readable content in print and PDF output.
